# Cppify

Welcome to **Cppify**! 🚀

Ever tried to learn C++ and felt like you were reading alien hieroglyphics translated by a malfunctioning robot? Yeah, me too. 

That's why I created this repo. It's a collection of C++ concepts (from absolute basics like Pointers up to the shiny magic of C++20) explained so simply that a 5th grader could understand it. 

We don't do "abstract mathematical theories" here. We do Piggy Banks, TV Remotes, Delivery Drivers, and Magic Bags.

## What's in the box? 📦

* `core_concepts/`: Pointers and Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) explained without the headache.
* `modern_cpp/`: The good stuff from C++11, C++17, and C++20. (Yes, we have Spaceship operators `<=>` 🚀).
* `multithreading/`: Trying to do 50 things at once without your computer exploding. 

## How to use this? 🛠️

Just read the code! Seriously. Every file is basically a mini-story wrapped in compile-ready C++ code. At the bottom of every file, there's an `EXPECTED OUTPUT` section so you don't even *have* to compile it if you're feeling lazy. (But you should. `g++ -std=c++20 filename.cpp` is your friend).

## Disclaimer ⚠️

If you are a hardcore C++ veteran looking for hyper-optimized template metaprogramming tricks, you will be deeply offended by my analogies. I am not sorry. 

Happy coding! 🎉
